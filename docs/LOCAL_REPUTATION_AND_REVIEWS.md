## 2. Review Generation Process (Authentic & Ethical)

> **Golden Rule**: Never buy reviews, never use automated bots, and never write fake testimonials. Authentic parent and student reviews create sustained local algorithmic trust.

### Key Milestones for Requesting Reviews:
1. **Post-Mock / Test Series Success**: When a student demonstrates marked improvement after 2-3 months of mentorship.
2. **Board Exam & Entrance Result Announcements**: When 10th SSC, 12th HSC, JEE, or MHT-CET results are declared.
3. **Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs)**: Handing parents a convenient QR code card at the reception.

### Direct Review Shortlink & QR Strategy:
- Generate a direct review link via GBP dashboard: `https://g.page/r/[YOUR_GBP_ID]/review`
- **SMS/WhatsApp Review Request Template**:
  > *"Dear [Parent/Student Name], thank you for being a part of Wavefront Academy. If you found our mentoring and faculty guidance helpful in your child's academic journey, we would truly appreciate a brief 1-minute review on Google to help other parents in Virar make the right choice: [GBP_REVIEW_LINK]. Thank you!"*
